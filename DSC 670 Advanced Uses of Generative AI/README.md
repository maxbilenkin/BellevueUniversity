# DSC 670 – Advanced Uses of Generative AI

## Course Overview
This course explores the application of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GAI) in real-world scenarios. Students learn to apply large language models (LLMs) to process text, generate insights, and create applications. Key topics include prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning, and deploying custom LLM-powered applications.

## Project: Using Generative AI to Summarize and Compare SEC 10-K Filings for Investment Research

### Project Description
Investment research is often time-consuming, with analysts reviewing 10-K filings that can exceed hundreds of pages. Traditional methods require strong financial expertise and substantial time, which can delay investment decisions.

This project leverages **Generative AI** to summarize, extract, and compare information from SEC 10-K filings. By using LLMs, analysts can efficiently process large volumes of text, identify key business risks, understand strategic priorities, and compare companies.

### Problem Statement
Originally focused on early-stage company analysis, the project was revised to align with generative AI capabilities. The updated goal is to support investment research tasks, specifically using LLMs to summarize, extract, and compare information from SEC 10-K filings for informed decision-making.

### Generative AI Approach
- **Core Technology:** Large Language Models (LLMs)  
- **Use Case:** Process and analyze natural language documents (10-K filings)  
- **Tasks Supported:** Summarization, risk extraction, strategic analysis, year-over-year comparisons, and company comparisons  

### Prompt Experiments
1. **Executive Summary** – Summarize a 10-K filing in plain English for a long-term investor.  
2. **Risk Extraction** – Identify and summarize the top five business risks.  
3. **Strategic Focus** – Highlight management priorities and examples from the filing.  
4. **Year-over-Year Comparison** – Compare this year’s filing to the previous year’s.  
5. **Company Comparison** – Compare filings of two companies, highlighting key differences in business model, risks, and competitive positioning.  

### Fine-Tuning Strategy
Future expansion could involve fine-tuning the LLM with curated historical 10-K filings. Focus areas would include business risks, management discussion, and competitive positioning to reduce generic outputs and increase company-specific insights.

### Limitations and Future Work
- LLMs may hallucinate or generate inaccurate financial interpretations.  
- Context length limits may restrict the amount of text processed at once.  
- Analysis is limited to publicly available information and cannot account for undisclosed risks.  
- Future enhancements could include improved prompts, document chunking, and human review integration.

## Quick Start

To run the **Investment Research Assistant**, follow these steps:

1. **Install Python 3.9+**  
   Download and install Python from [python.org](https://www.python.org/).

2. **Install required packages**
```bash
pip install streamlit openai
````

3. **Set your OpenAI API key**

   * **Windows (PowerShell):**

```powershell
setx OPENAI_API_KEY "your_key"
```

* **macOS / Linux:**

```bash
export OPENAI_API_KEY="your_key"
```

4. **Open a terminal and navigate to the project folder**

```bash
cd path/to/your/project
```

5. **Launch the Streamlit app**

```bash
streamlit run app.py
```

6. **Open the app in your browser**
   The app will open automatically at [http://localhost:8501/](http://localhost:8501/). If not, enter the URL manually.

7. **Enter prompts about SEC 10-K filings**
   Use the input box in the app to type questions or commands, for example:

   * Summarize this 10-K for a long-term investor
   * Extract top 5 risks from the filing
   * Compare this filing to last year’s

8. **Click "Generate Response"**
   The app will provide analyst-style summaries, risk assessments, strategic comparisons, and other insights generated by the LLM.

## Impact & Insights

* Accelerates investment research by summarizing large filings in seconds.
* Supports identification of business risks, management strategies, and competitive positioning.
* Allows analysts to focus on high-level decision-making rather than manual reading.
* Demonstrates a practical application of generative AI in financial analysis workflows.
