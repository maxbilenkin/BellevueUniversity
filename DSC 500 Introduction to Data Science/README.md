# DSC 500 – Introduction to Data Science

## Course Overview
This course introduces the possibilities, history, and ethical considerations surrounding Data Science. It explores fundamental concepts including data science terminology, programming languages, big data frameworks, data visualization, and basic statistical concepts. Prior programming experience was not required for this course.

## Course Project
For the final project, I explored the topic:

**"How to Achieve a Century-Long Life?"**

The objective of this project was to investigate potential common patterns among centenarians and identify lifestyle habits that may contribute to longevity. The research focused on exploring behaviors, lifestyle choices, and patterns that might influence people living to 100 years or more.

## Data Exploration
To support the analysis, I used **Google Trends search interest data** to examine how global interest in longevity and living a long life has changed over time. The research included:

- Trends in search interest over time
- Regional comparisons of search interest across different parts of the world
- Visualizations such as line charts and regional maps obtained from Google Trends

These visualizations were incorporated directly into the research paper to support the analysis.

## Deliverables
The final project included:

- Research paper written in **APA format**
- Data visualizations embedded within the research paper
- **PowerPoint presentation** summarizing the research findings

## Skills Demonstrated
- Exploratory research
- Data interpretation
- Data visualization
- Communicating analytical insights through written reports
- Presentation of research findings
